## **The Challenges with Existing Tools**

If you've ever been part of an Agile team, you’re probably familiar with **Planning Poker**, the consensus-based method for estimating the effort required to complete user stories or tasks. While the method is effective, the tools available to facilitate it were often clunky or required sign-ups, subscriptions, and unnecessary features. I often found myself bogged down by the logistics, which took away from the value of the activity itself.

Similarly, **Retrospectives**, another crucial aspect of Scrum, often felt like a logistical nightmare. Teams need a way to share anonymous feedback, but many tools either lacked that feature or were too complicated for quick, meaningful feedback collection. What I wanted was a tool that made it simple to facilitate **retrospectives** and **Planning Poker** without the usual complexity or cost.

## **The Birth of scrum.host: A Free Planning Poker Tool**

After experiencing these frustrations, I decided to build **scrum.host**, a **free planning poker** and **free retrospective** tool that solves these problems while remaining simple, intuitive, and accessible. **scrum.host** is designed to meet the needs of Scrum teams by providing:

*   **Free Planning Poker**: A simple and effective way to estimate the complexity of tasks without any registration or setup.
